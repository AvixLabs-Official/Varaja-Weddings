FEATURED EVENT OF THE SEASON

The Wedding of
Ananya & Arjun

Kolkata • Traditional Royal Bengali Heritage Wedding

Set amidst a 150-year-old historic Rajbari in Kolkata, this multi-day wedding managed by BLUE ROSE PRODUCTION blended authentic Zamindari traditions with high-fashion production design. Over 10,000 fresh tuberose strands, illuminated crystal chandeliers, and classical Shehnai recitals created an unforgettable royal atmosphere.

📍 Venue: Taj Bengal & Heritage Rajbari
👥 Guests: 450 Overseas & VIP Guests
🌸 Decor: Dutch White Roses & Tuberose
🎶 Music: Live Classical Sitar & Shehnai
